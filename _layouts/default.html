Heng Chang

changh17 at tsinghua dot org dot cn

I am the Chief AI Scientist at Unity China, where I lead research and development in AI agents and foundation models. I was a senior researcher and assistant chief expert at Huawei of the TopMinds program. I received my Ph.D. degree with honors from Tsinghua-Berkeley Shenzhen Institute, Tsinghua University co-advised by Prof. Wenwu Zhu and Prof. Somayeh Sojoudi. I received my Bachelor's Degrees in the Department of Electronic Engineering and the School of Economics and Management from Tsinghua University in 2017. In the past, I was also fortunate to work with Prof. Stratos Idreos as an undergraduate research intern at DASLab in Harvard in 2016, with Yu Rong and Prof. Junzhou Huang as a PhD research intern at Tencent AI Lab in 2018, 2020 and 2021, and with Zhiqiang Zhang as a PhD research intern at Ant Group in 2021. I was also a ZhenIntern at ZhenFund in 2022.

Email  /  GitHub  /  Google Scholar  /  LinkedIn

Research Vision

I aim to build adaptive, reliable AI agents by combining foundation models with structured knowledge. My research spans LLM post-training and agent learning, knowledge-augmented generation, and generative recommendation and retrieval, building on earlier work in graph representation learning and trustworthy AI.
